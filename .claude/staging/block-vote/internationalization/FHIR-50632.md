# FHIR-50632: Remove relevant laws section

**JIRA:** https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-50632
**Status:** Triaged | **Resolution:** None
**Ballot:** may-2025 | **Section:** 1.3
**Proposed Resolution:** Persuasive with Modification
**Block Vote:** Internationalization (Block A) — with FHIR-50394, FHIR-50740, FHIR-50749

## Ballot Comment
Laws listed are US-specific and lack context explaining their relevance. Commenter recommends either clarifying how each law applies or removing the section entirely.

## Proposed Action
Rather than removing entirely, restructure as an international table:
1. Convert flat US-only list into a multi-jurisdiction table (US, EU, Japan columns)
2. Add brief annotation for each law explaining its relevance to PHR
3. Add introductory text: "PHR systems operate under different regulatory frameworks depending on jurisdiction. The following table provides a non-exhaustive reference."

## Affected Files
- `input/pagecontent/index.md` — Rewrite Section 1.3 "Relevant Law" (lines ~19-40)

## Implementation Notes
- "With Modification" because we restructure rather than remove
- The commenter's core concern (lack of context) is valid — each law entry should explain WHY it matters to PHR
- Current laws listed: HIPAA, 21st Century Cures, ONC HTI-1, CMS Interoperability, Information Blocking
- New additions needed: GDPR, EHDS Regulation, Japan PHR Business Association Act
