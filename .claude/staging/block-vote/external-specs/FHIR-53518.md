# FHIR-53518: mHealth harmonization

**JIRA:** https://jira.hl7.org/browse/FHIR-53518
**Status:** Submitted | **Resolution:** None
**Source:** PHR Council meeting
**Proposed Resolution:** Needs Workgroup Discussion

## Ballot Comment
mHealth harmonization — ensuring PHR IG aligns with mobile health standards and initiatives. Debatable approach; Hisashi/Fujitsu are open to discuss and have opinions.

## Proposed Action
Present to workgroup for discussion. Key questions:
1. Which mHealth standards should the PHR IG reference? (IEEE, Continua, FHIR mHealth)
2. How does the PGHD (Patient-Generated Health Data) section already cover mHealth?
3. Should there be a dedicated mHealth section or integrated references?

Options:
- **Option A:** Add mHealth references to conformance.md alongside other IGs
- **Option B:** Add a dedicated mHealth harmonization subsection to the supplemental pages
- **Option C:** Acknowledge mHealth overlap in PGHD section documentation
